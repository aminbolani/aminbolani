About My Work

Simple story, honest work, and custom web designs.

Hi, I'm Amin 👋

I have been working in web design and custom layout development for a long time. Throughout this journey, I have built websites for various businesses, but I particularly specialize in designing pages for service-based businesses — such as salons, barbershops, and local service providers.

I am genuinely passionate about my work, and every new project brings a valuable learning opportunity. My primary focus is always to deliver websites that are clean, fast-loading, and effortlessly communicate the core services to visitors.

My greatest strength lies in breaking down complex client requirements into simple, efficient, and user-friendly solutions. Completing projects neatly without unnecessary complexity is my standard practice.
