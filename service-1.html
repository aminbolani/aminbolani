UI/UX Figma Design

My Design Process

1

Requirement Analysis

First, I carefully review and analyze the client's requirements to thoroughly understand the project goals, brand vision, and target audience.

2

Paper Sketch & Layout Wireframe

Based on the requirements, I create a initial hand-drawn paper sketch to map out the core structure, section hierarchy, and layout flow for client review.

3

AI & JPG Visual Concept

Using AI tools and graphic software, I design a full JPG visual concept mock-up to give the client a clear presentation of how the final design aesthetics will look.

4

Pixel-Perfect Figma Handoff

Upon final approval, I translate the complete concept into Figma using modern UI standards, auto-layouts, clean components, and developer-ready design systems.

  • High-Resolution JPG / PNG Preview Mockups
